The [original grant](../LICENSES/LicenseRef-Aeries-Playground-Open-Source-1.0.txt) remains authoritative if the reading edition differs from it. Both are included in the opening license comment of [the userscript](../aeries-playground.user.js), so downloading that one file also supplies the complete original terms.

## Review the complete source

You may give **the entire source**, including private changes, to ChatGPT, another AI service or model, an automated scanner, or a human reviewer for explanation, analysis, debugging, testing, or security auditing. A commercial provider or paid reviewer is allowed. The permission is not limited to short excerpts.

The original grant directly permits reviewers, service providers, and their processors to receive, copy, store, process, and analyze submitted code; make working changes; produce results; retain review records; and return reviewed code, patches, or proposed changes. No additional permission from a Licensor is needed for those activities.

Private review does not require publishing submitted code, changes, or review records. A recipient still receives the applicable license rights, and distributing Software or modified copies remains subject to the same distribution conditions. The license does not itself require a provider to keep code confidential or delete records. Check a provider's handling of material before submitting private changes; do not assume paid review removes the recipient's license rights.

For reviewing Playground itself, send the userscript with its license comment. You do not need a real Aeries page, account access, grades, passwords, cookies, or student records. The software license supplies no rights in that private material. Use fictional inputs when a reviewer needs an example, and follow [Security and privacy](../SECURITY.md) for reporting a vulnerability.

### Findings, patches, and AI outputs

A report written independently remains the report author's material. It does not acquire this license just because it describes Playground. Code copied or derived from Playground remains subject to the license to the extent copyright applies; placing it in a report or receiving it from an AI tool does not erase those terms. The license does not decide whether a particular output is copyrightable, determine who owns a provider's output, or replace the provider's separate terms.

## Share copies and modifications

The same conditions apply to paid and unpaid copies, public forks, mirrors, bundles, and copies supplied privately. A fee for a copy does not remove the recipient's right to share it under the license.

1. **Include the complete original license and existing notices.** A repository copy includes [LICENSE](../LICENSE) and the [original-grant file](../LICENSES/LicenseRef-Aeries-Playground-Open-Source-1.0.txt). The userscript already embeds both. Retaining those files or that complete comment is a straightforward way to carry the terms and notices with the code.
2. **Identify modifications.** Mark a changed version as modified, describe the changes briefly, and give their dates. Preserve existing attribution, and add accurate notices for your own contributions. Do not present a modification as an official release or imply a Licensor's endorsement.
3. **Keep covered modifications under Version 1.0.** Recipients obtain their permissions directly from the relevant copyright holders. Do not add legal or contractual restrictions on exercising those rights.
4. **Provide complete corresponding source.** Every recipient must receive, or be able to obtain without an additional charge, the preferred form for changing the exact version supplied.

### What counts as corresponding source

For this project's ordinary readable userscript distribution, the installable file is the source. Keep its complete license comment. If you create a build step or another distributed form, provide your changes and the project-specific files or scripts needed to build, install, and run that version. Minified or deliberately obscured code and generated intermediate output cannot replace the preferred form for making changes.

General-purpose tools and unchanged dependencies that are ordinarily available separately need not be included under this requirement; their own license obligations still apply. A dependency's availability does not authorize removing its notices or ignoring its terms.

You may include source with the copy or give clear instructions for obtaining it by a readily accessible means without an additional charge. Maintain that means while you distribute that version. A link to changing `main` is sufficient only while it supplies the matching source and remains accessible; use the exact version's source when distributing an older or modified copy. The license does not add a fixed multi-year hosting period.

### Private use and services

You can keep modifications private when you merely run the script, including to provide a service. If you give another person a copy, including during private review, the original distribution terms still apply. The source obligation concerns those recipients and does not require publishing source to everyone. This license does not promise confidentiality or let a distributor cancel compliant recipients' directly granted rights.
